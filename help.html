Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to the Queue page from the sidebar. You'll see the current patient being served at the top. Click the large green "Call Next" button to advance to the next patient. The patient's token number will be announced on the display screen in the waiting area. You can also Skip a patient or Recall the current patient.
When you toggle your status to "Emergency Ward", the queue is automatically paused. A notification banner appears for all waiting patients indicating that the doctor is in the emergency ward. You will not be able to call next patients until you switch back to "In OPD" status. The same applies to Rounds and Break statuses.
The AI Summary is automatically generated when a patient is called from the queue. It pulls data from the patient's triage notes (symptoms, vitals entered by the nurse), past visit history, known allergies, and chronic conditions. The summary is designed to save you 2-3 minutes per consultation by providing a quick overview before the patient sits down. You can disable this feature from Profile → Prescription Defaults.
The checkbox interface shows medicines currently stocked in the government pharmacy. If you need to prescribe a medicine not in the list, you can use the Advice/Instructions text area at the bottom of the prescription page to write custom medicines or provide instructions for the patient to purchase from an outside pharmacy.
After selecting medicines, dosage, and duration on the Prescription page, click "Generate & Print". A print-friendly prescription will be generated with the SGHMS header, patient details, diagnosis, medicines, and your signature. The browser's print dialog will open automatically. Use A5 paper size for OPD prescription slips.
Drug interaction alerts are triggered when two prescribed medicines may cause adverse effects when taken together. Review the alert carefully — it will tell you the specific risk (e.g., increased bleeding, kidney damage). You can choose to proceed with caution, adjust the dose, or substitute one of the medicines. All alerts are logged in the Alerts page for audit purposes.
